06 Policy Alignment

Aligned with Oman Vision 2040

A mandatory SSR standard is not a stand-alone safety fix — it is a direct expression of national priorities Oman has already set for itself.

Two priorities, one proposal

The project aligns closely with Oman Vision 2040, particularly its priorities on the Legislative, Judicial, and Oversight System, and on Well-Being and Social Protection. Mandating SSR turns a safety recommendation into enforceable regulation — and protects the people whose livelihoods depend on Oman's quarries.

Legislative, Judicial & Oversight System Well-Being & Social Protection

What the mandate does

  • Moves SSR from a discretionary safety measure to an enforceable regulatory standard, overseen by the Ministry of Minerals and Energy.
  • Extends social protection to quarry workers, whose safety currently rests on hazard management that has already failed once, in Ibri.
  • Establishes a pilot-then-scale model: prove the standard in Ibri, then extend it to every hazardous quarry in Oman.
Earning public trust

Bringing the community along

Regulation alone does not build confidence. Awareness sessions across Ibri — for workers, operators, and their families — are part of the plan, so that the people closest to the risk understand how the system protects them and what to do when it triggers an alarm.
