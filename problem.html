01 The Problem

A town at the mountain's edge

Ibri is growing fast, sits beside some of Oman's busiest quarries, and has already paid the price of hazard management gone wrong.

Where Ibri sits

Ibri is a wilayat in the northwest of Al-Dhahirah governorate, Oman, at the edge of the Al-Hajar mountains — approximately 300 km from Muscat. Ibri is also home to many of Oman's premier quarrying companies, which makes slope safety in the surrounding hills a matter of direct, daily consequence for the people who work there.

Growth pressure

A population set to nearly double

Ibri has a population of 71,000 as of 2024, projected to reach 120,000 by 2040. More residents means more workers, families, and daily life pressed up against the same quarries and slopes — raising the stakes for getting hazard management right now, not later.

The Ibri rockslide

A quarry landslide in Ibri claimed the lives of 13 workers. It illustrates the serious consequences of inadequate hazard management, and underscores the need for improved safety measures across Oman's quarries.
