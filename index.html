The proposal

One missing layer of defence

This project proposes making Slope Stability Radar (SSR) mandatory in every hazardous quarry in Oman — starting with the wilayat of Ibri as a pilot case to demonstrate the system's effectiveness before wider adoption across the country.

SSR continuously measures the movement and acceleration of rock material to detect developing instability, and triggers an alarm to warn workers before a slope failure occurs — turning an unpredictable mountain into a monitored one.
